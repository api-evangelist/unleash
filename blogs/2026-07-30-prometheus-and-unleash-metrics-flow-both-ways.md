If you run a platform team, you already have a metrics stack. Prometheus (or something that speaks its API, like VictoriaMetrics) is scraping your services, Grafana is drawing the dashboards, and your on-call rotation lives in those graphs. The last thing you want from a feature flag tool is another silo.
